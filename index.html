Everything to choose from for dinner for Macho, Verne, and Michael — descriptions and ingredients for each option so we can pick together.

Gluten-free note for Macho: Goop Kitchen states in its official allergen guide that the whole kitchen is gluten-free — but it's not celiac-safe, since some locations share prep space with other allergens. Every item below is gluten-free unless flagged otherwise. If Macho has celiac disease (not just a gluten sensitivity), it's worth a quick call/text to the store (310-954-1286) before ordering.
Garlic note for Michael: Anything below tagged "Contains Garlic" lists garlic as a main ingredient (not just a background seasoning) — worth avoiding or checking on given the garlic sensitivity.
